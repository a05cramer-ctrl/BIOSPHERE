    how the dome works

    three rules. no referee.

    01

    hatch

    A coin's address seeds its creature: species, colour, appetite and stamina all come from the hash. Same coin, same creature, every time.

    02

    feed or starve

    Every buy is food and the creature grows. Every sell makes it bleed and shrink. Sixty minutes with no trade and it starves, leaving a sprout in the soil.

    03

    crown

    Once an hour the dome measures everything alive. The biggest creature is apex and wears the crown until something out-eats it.

    the food chain

    five species. one soil.

    Which one a coin hatches as is decided by its address, not by how it trades. Trading only decides how big it gets.

    Mossling

    Slow, round, lives on the soil. The steady grazer: small buys, all day.

    "never fast. never hungry."

    Pip

    The hatchling. Tiny, loud, moves a lot. Most coins arrive as a Pip and most Pips don't see the crown.

    "just hatched, already hungry."

    Snail

    Carries its shell. Bleeds slowly, starves slowly. The holder's creature.

    "an hour is nothing."

    Jelly

    Floats above the soil. Balloons on a feed, deflates on a bleed. Pure volatility.

    "up is the only direction i know."

    Beetle

    Armoured and quick. Eats whatever is dropped first. Lives on volume, dies without it.

    "first to the food."
